Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 2, 2026

Public Sentiment Dash respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected from website visitors, registered members, and future mobile-app users; how it is used; and the choices available to users.

Public Sentiment Dash is informational and educational only. It does not provide financial, investment, trading, tax, or legal advice.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect your email address, encrypted authentication credentials, optional display name, account identifiers, confirmation and recovery status, and account creation or sign-in timestamps. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not visible to Public Sentiment Dash in readable form.
  • Member settings and content: We may store preferences, theme and timeframe choices, watchlists, alert rules, notification preferences, device-notification tokens, and account-deletion requests.
  • Limited usage analytics: With permission, we may record the page viewed, navigation link used, feature or button used, form name submitted, timestamp, and whether the activity came from a registered member or guest.
  • What is not sent to analytics: We do not send names, email addresses, readable account IDs, passwords, typed form contents, search text, watchlist contents, alert contents, contact messages, or voting identifiers to anonymous aggregate usage counters.
  • Standard analytics data: anonymous aggregate usage counters may also process standard pseudonymous information such as a browser cookie, session statistics, approximate location, browser, and device category after anonymous aggregate measurement is granted.
  • Anonymous voting: The selected instrument, vote direction, date, and a browser-based voter identifier may be stored to display aggregated sentiment and reduce duplicate voting.
  • Contact information: Information you voluntarily submit through a contact form is used to respond to your request.
  • Cookies and browser storage: We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies for authentication, preferences, voting, analytics choices, security, and basic functionality.
  • Hosting and security logs: Hosting and infrastructure providers may process IP address, user agent, request time, requested page, and related technical information for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

How We Use Information

  • Provide accounts, recovery, preferences, watchlists, alerts, and synchronized website/app features.
  • Understand which pages and features are useful to members and guests.
  • Improve navigation, content, performance, accessibility, and product design.
  • Measure aggregate traffic and feature adoption by members and guests.
  • Operate anonymous voting and display aggregate results.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, fraud, security incidents, and technical failures.
  • Respond to messages and comply with legal obligations.

Analytics and Privacy Choices

Optional analytics are controlled through the website’s analytics banner and “Privacy Choices” control. A registered member may also use the analytics opt-out setting in the account page. Declining analytics does not prevent essential authentication, security, preference, or voting functions from operating.

Analytics reports are intended to be aggregate. Public Sentiment Dash sends only a “member” or “guest” classification for comparison and does not send a member’s email, name, readable account ID, or saved personal content to analytics.

Advertising

The website and future app may display advertising. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies subject to user consent and their own policies. We intend to keep advertising separate from account credentials and private member settings. Public Sentiment Dash does not sell personal information for money.

Third-Party Service Providers

Service providers may process information only as needed to provide their services. Current or planned providers may include anonymous aggregate usage counters and advertising services, Supabase for authentication and database services, GitHub or Cloudflare for hosting and content delivery, Formspree or similar form services, and Apple or Google for future mobile distribution and notifications.

External websites and articles have their own privacy practices. Public Sentiment Dash is not responsible for third-party websites.

Data Retention

We retain information only as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, account operation, security, backups, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. Account information and personalized settings may be retained while an account is active and during processing of a deletion request. Analytics retention is controlled through the analytics provider’s settings. Local browser identifiers remain until they expire or are cleared.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as encrypted connections, managed authentication, access controls, and database security policies. No online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices and Requests

  • Accept, decline, or later change anonymous aggregate activity measurement choices.
  • Update account profile, preferences, watchlists, and notification settings.
  • Request password recovery through your registered email.
  • Request account and personalized-data deletion through the account page or contact page.
  • Clear cookies or local storage through browser controls.
  • Where applicable law provides additional access, correction, deletion, or opt-out rights, submit a request through the contact page.

Children’s Privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users under the age of legal majority should use the service only with appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

International Visitors

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where service providers operate. Privacy protections may differ from those in your country.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as the website, app, providers, or legal requirements change. The updated date will appear at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the service.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the Public Sentiment Dash contact page.
