What Is Market Sentiment?

Market sentiment describes the overall public tone around a market, asset, or theme. It reflects whether current discussion and coverage are mainly bullish, bearish, mixed, or neutral.

Sentiment can be influenced by economic data, company results, policy decisions, regulation, geopolitical events, market volatility, expectations, fear, confidence, and changing risk appetite.

Public Sentiment Dash organizes those signals into a clearer view. It is designed to provide context—not to predict prices or replace technical, fundamental, or risk analysis.

How to Use Public Sentiment Dash

  1. Start with the Interactive Dashboard or choose the market category you want to study.
  2. Review the current sentiment reading and the main public drivers behind it.
  3. Use Historical Sentiment, Compare, and Market Charts to check whether the narrative and price direction agree or diverge.
  4. Use the information as research context and make decisions based on your own analysis and risk management.
